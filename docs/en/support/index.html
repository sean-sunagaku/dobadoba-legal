How to play

  • Hold the screen and balls pour out of the nozzle.
  • Slide your finger left and right while holding to move the nozzle.
  • Balls multiply when they pass through gates such as “×2” and “×3”.
  • Balls disappear when they pass through a red “✕” gate.
  • You earn 1 to 3 stars depending on how many balls land in the bucket. One star clears the stage.
  • A timer starts when you first pour. When time is up, the remaining balls cannot be poured.

FAQ

There is no sound.
Check that the silent switch is off and the volume is not zero. Also check that Sound is turned on in Settings in the App.

I want to start over.
Go to Settings → Reset progress to erase your stars and records. This cannot be undone.

My records disappeared after changing devices.
Records are stored only on the device. They carry over when you restore from an iPhone backup, but not when you simply reinstall the App.

About ads.
To keep the game free, banner ads appear on the home and result screens, and full-screen ads occasionally appear between stages. When you fail a stage, you can watch a video ad to add balls and continue (this is optional).

Which devices are supported?
iPhone with iOS 17.0 or later.
